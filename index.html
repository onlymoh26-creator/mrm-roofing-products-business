Roofing solutions built around quality and innovation.

Whether you are planning a new home, upgrading an existing roof or working on a commercial development, explore the available roofing options and identify products that suit your project requirements.

CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about roofing products and ordering.

What roofing products are available?

The four main categories shown are Box Profile, Tile Profile, Stone-Coated Roofing and Corrugated Roofing.

Concealed-fix and specialised profiles are listed separately as a subsection. Confirm product availability and specifications when enquiring.

How do I choose the right roofing sheets?

Consider your building design, roof structure, preferred appearance, required sheet dimensions, finish and budget. A roofing quotation can help establish suitable product specifications.

How can I get current product prices?

Use the product calculators for the rates displayed on this website, or generate a pro-forma invoice through the roofing quotation section to review the applicable product prices.

Can I request a roofing quotation?

Yes. Open the roofing quotation section, enter your customer and delivery details, select your products and specifications, and generate your pro-forma invoice.

You can download the quotation, view it online or share its link through WhatsApp.

Do you have roofing accessories?

The catalogue includes common roofing accessories such as ridge caps, valley trays, flashings, fasteners, gutters, downpipes and ventilation items. Confirm availability when enquiring.

Are different roofing colours available?

Colour options vary depending on the roofing product, finish and specifications. Contact the team to confirm available colours.

How do the product calculators work?

Select a gauge where applicable and enter the required length or number of pieces. The calculator multiplies the displayed rate by the selected quantity.

These calculators estimate material cost only. They do not calculate roof area, sheet quantities, accessories or delivery.

How do I place an order?

Generate your pro-forma invoice using the roofing quotation section. Share the quotation link with the MRM team through WhatsApp for assistance with the next steps.
